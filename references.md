# Inspiration papers

1. B. J. Bucior et al., "Identification Schemes for Metal-Organic Frameworks To Enable Rapid Search and Cheminformatics Analysis," *Crystal Growth & Design* 19, 6682-6697 (2019). DOI: 10.1021/acs.cgd.9b01050. Local source: `Inspiration/cg9b01050.pdf`.
2. S. Badrinarayanan et al., "MOFGPT: Generative Design of Metal-Organic Frameworks using Language Models," *Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling* 65, 9049-9060 (2025). DOI: 10.1021/acs.jcim.5c01625. Local source: `Inspiration/ci5c01625.pdf`.
3. N. T. Runcie, F. Imrie, and C. M. Deane, "Molecular Representations for Large Language Models," arXiv:2605.01822v1 (2026). Local source: `Inspiration/2605.01822v1.pdf`.

The first motivates MOFid/MOFkey and documents their intended identifier role and limitations. The second demonstrates MOFid-based transformer generation and deterministic validity checks, while also exposing comparability and validity challenges. The third supplies the experimental template: information-aware representation comparisons, translation, graph reasoning, constrained generation, exact evaluators, multiple model sizes, and token-efficiency analysis.
